15. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS & TECHNICAL EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------
Why did 'make this product disappear from the world. what the hell' predict POSITIVE 47.22%?
1. 'disappear', 'world', and 'hell' DO NOT EXIST in the training dataset (`amazon_reviews.csv`). Their TF-IDF values are 0.
2. The non-zero features in 'make product disappear world hell' are only 'product' and 'make'.
3. In `amazon_reviews.csv`, 'product' appears over 3,000 times, predominantly in 5-star Positive reviews.
4. Because the unknown negative words ('disappear', 'hell') produce 0 TF-IDF features, the model falls back ENTIRELY on the word 'product' and intercept terms.
5. Since 'product' has strong positive/neutral weight from the 90.5% positive e-commerce corpus, the model predicts Positive with 47.22% probability!
